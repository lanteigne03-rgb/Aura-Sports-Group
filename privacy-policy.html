This Privacy Policy ("Policy") describes the categories of information Aura collects through aurasportsgroup.com and its associated pages (the "Site"), the purposes for which such information is used and disclosed, and the choices available to you. 1. Information We Collect

Aura collects information about you in the following ways:

Information You Provide Directly

When you submit an inquiry through the Contact page or a comparable form, Aura collects the information you enter, which may include your name, email address, telephone number, the category that best describes you (for example, NFL player, college player, recruit, parent or family member, or coach), your school or team, your position, and any additional information you elect to provide. If you contact Aura by email, telephone, or in person, Aura collects the information you share in the course of that communication.

Information Collected Automatically

As is standard practice for most websites, Aura's hosting and analytics providers may automatically record certain technical information when you access the Site, including your IP address, browser and device type, the pages viewed, and referring pages, for purposes of security, performance monitoring, and analytics. Section 5 below describes Aura's use of cookies and similar technologies in greater detail.

Information Obtained from Other Sources

In the event you become a client of Aura, Aura may separately obtain additional information concerning you from coaches, teams, schools, scouting and personnel departments, leagues, and other contacts within the football industry in connection with its representation of you. Such information is governed by the terms of the applicable representation agreement and not by this Policy.

2. Recruits, Minors & Families

Aura's Recruits program is directed toward high school student-athletes, a number of whom are minors. If you are under the age of eighteen (18), Aura requests that a parent or legal guardian review this Policy and submit any inquiry on your behalf, or jointly with you. By designating "Recruit" or otherwise identifying a minor in connection with an inquiry, the submitting adult represents and warrants that they possess the authority to disclose that minor's information to Aura for the purpose of discussing representation and related services.

Aura uses information concerning recruits solely to respond to the applicable inquiry and, where a family elects to proceed, to discuss and formalize representation. Aura does not use such information for unrelated marketing directed toward minors.

Aura uses the information it collects for the following purposes:

  • To respond to inquiries and communicate with you regarding representation, marketing, or brand opportunities;
  • To evaluate prospective clients and to prepare for and conduct representation, where a relationship is formed;
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Site, including troubleshooting and security;
  • To understand how visitors use the Site in order to improve its content and performance;
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations, enforce Aura's agreements, and protect the rights, safety, and property of Aura, its clients, and others; and
  • With your consent, to send you updates concerning Aura, subject to any opt-out you exercise.

  • Legal and safety authorities, where required by law, regulation, or legal process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Aura, its clients, or others; and

Should Aura implement analytics, advertising, or other tracking technologies in the future, this Policy will be updated accordingly and, where required by applicable law, a cookie notice or consent mechanism will be provided. Aura retains information obtained through website inquiries for so long as is necessary to respond to you, evaluate a potential relationship, and comply with its legal, accounting, and record-keeping obligations, following which such information is deleted or anonymized.
