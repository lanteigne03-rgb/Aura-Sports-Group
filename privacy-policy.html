1. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following ways:

Information you provide to us

When you submit an inquiry through our Contact page or a similar form, we collect what you enter, which may include your name, email address, phone number, the role that best describes you (for example, NFL player, college player, recruit, parent or family member, or coach), your school or team, your position, and any message or details you choose to share. If you contact us by email, phone, or at an event, we collect what you share with us in that conversation.

Information we collect automatically

Like most websites, our hosting and analytics providers may automatically log standard technical information when you visit the Site — such as IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed, and referring pages — for security, performance, and analytics purposes. See Section 5 for more on cookies and similar technologies.

Information from other sources

If you become a client, we may separately collect additional information about you from coaches, teams, schools, scouting and personnel departments, leagues, and other football-industry contacts as part of representing you — that information is governed by your representation agreement, not this website Privacy Policy.

2. Recruits, Minors & Families

Our Recruits pathway is directed at high school student-athletes, many of whom are minors. If you are under 18, please have a parent or legal guardian review this Policy and submit any inquiry on your behalf, or submit it together with you. By selecting "Recruit" or otherwise identifying a minor in an inquiry, the submitting adult confirms they have the authority to share that minor's information with us for the purpose of discussing representation and related services.

We use information about recruits solely to respond to the inquiry and, where a family chooses to move forward, to discuss and document representation — not for unrelated marketing to minors.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to inquiries and communicate with you about representation, marketing, or brand opportunities;
  • Evaluate prospective clients and prepare for and conduct representation, where a relationship is formed;
  • Operate, maintain, and improve the Site, including troubleshooting and security;
  • Understand how visitors use the Site so we can improve its content and performance;
  • Comply with legal obligations, enforce our agreements, and protect the rights, safety, and property of Aura, our clients, and others; and
  • With your consent, send you updates about Aura, subject to any opt-out you exercise.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Service providers who perform services on our behalf, such as email delivery, form processing, website hosting, and analytics, under obligations to protect your information;
  • Industry contacts — such as teams, leagues, schools, brands, and other parties relevant to your representation or inquiry — but only as needed and, where practical, with your involvement or consent;
  • Professional advisors, such as our attorneys, accountants, and auditors, as needed;
  • Legal and safety authorities, where required by law, regulation, legal process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Aura, our clients, or others; and
  • A successor entity, in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of some or all of our business.

5. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

The Site currently loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may involve a connection to Google's servers when the page loads. We do not currently run advertising cookies or third-party analytics trackers on the Site. If we add analytics, advertising, or other tracking technologies in the future, we will update this Policy to describe them and, where required by law, provide a cookie notice or consent mechanism. Most browsers let you block or delete cookies through their settings; doing so may affect how parts of the Site function.

6. Data Retention

We retain information from website inquiries for as long as needed to respond to you, evaluate a potential relationship, and comply with our legal, accounting, and record-keeping obligations, after which we delete or anonymize it. Information collected in connection with a client representation relationship is retained under the terms of that agreement and applicable professional and regulatory requirements.

7. Your Choices & Rights

You may ask us to access, correct, update, or delete the personal information we hold about you by contacting us using the details in Section 13. You may also opt out of marketing communications at any time by following the unsubscribe instructions in an email or by contacting us directly.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act (as amended), other U.S. state privacy laws, or the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation — including the right to know what information we hold, request deletion, correct inaccuracies, and not be discriminated against for exercising these rights. We do not sell or "share" personal information as those terms are defined under applicable law. We will respond to verifiable requests consistent with applicable law.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect information submitted through the Site. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please avoid sending highly sensitive information (such as government ID numbers or financial account details) through the contact form.

9. Third-Party Links

The Site links to third-party sites, including news coverage and social media platforms. Those sites have their own privacy practices, and this Policy does not apply to them. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of any third-party site you visit.

10. Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental involvement. Section 2 above describes how we handle inquiries involving minors between 13 and 17. If we learn that we have collected personal information directly from a child under 13 without appropriate parental consent, we will take steps to delete it.

11. International Visitors

The Site is operated in the United States and intended for U.S. visitors. If you access the Site from outside the United States, your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States, which may have different data protection laws than your home country.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the revised version on this page with an updated "Last updated" date, and where changes are material, we will provide additional notice as appropriate.
